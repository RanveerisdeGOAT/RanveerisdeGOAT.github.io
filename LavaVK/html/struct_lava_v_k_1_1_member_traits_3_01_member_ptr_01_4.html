Detailed Description

template<typename M, typename S, M S::* MemberPtr>
struct LavaVK::MemberTraits< MemberPtr >

Compile-time helper that extracts the byte offset of a struct member from its pointer-to-member.

Specializes MemberTraits for the case where MemberPtr is a pointer to a member M of struct S. Used by #VertexLayout::attribute() to compute a vertex attribute's byte offset directly from a &Vertex::field expression, without the caller needing offsetof.
