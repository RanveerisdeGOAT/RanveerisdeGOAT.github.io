Detailed Description

Clean C++ encapsulation of VkSurfaceCapabilitiesKHR.

Queried from a physical device / surface pair to determine valid swapchain image counts, extents, and the formats and present modes the surface supports. Used by SwapChain when selecting swapchain creation parameters.
