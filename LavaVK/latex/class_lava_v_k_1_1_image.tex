\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Represents a Vulkan image and its associated image view. 

An \doxylink{class_lava_v_k_1_1_image}{Image} may either own its underlying Vk\+Image and allocated memory, or simply wrap an externally created image (such as a swapchain image).
