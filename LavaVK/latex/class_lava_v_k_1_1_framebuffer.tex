\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Represents a Vulkan framebuffer. 

A framebuffer is a collection of image attachments compatible with a specific \doxylink{class_lava_v_k_1_1_render_pass}{Render\+Pass}. Attachments typically include a color image, depth image, or additional render targets.
