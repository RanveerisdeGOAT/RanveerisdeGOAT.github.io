\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Represents a Vulkan framebuffer. 

A framebuffer is a collection of image attachments compatible with a specific \doxylink{class_lava_v_k_1_1_render_pass}{Render\+Pass}. Attachments typically include a color image, depth image, or additional render targets. {\ttfamily \doxylink{class_lava_v_k_1_1_framebuffer}{Framebuffer}} creates the underlying {\ttfamily Vk\+Framebuffer} in its constructor from a list of \doxylink{class_lava_v_k_1_1_image}{Image} attachments and a compatible \doxylink{class_lava_v_k_1_1_render_pass}{Render\+Pass}, and destroys it in the destructor. It does not own the {\ttfamily \doxylink{class_lava_v_k_1_1_image}{Image}} attachments themselves — those must outlive the {\ttfamily \doxylink{class_lava_v_k_1_1_framebuffer}{Framebuffer}}.
