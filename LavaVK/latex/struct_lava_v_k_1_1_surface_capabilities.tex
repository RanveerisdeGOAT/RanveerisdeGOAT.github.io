\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Clean C++ encapsulation of {\ttfamily Vk\+Surface\+Capabilities\+KHR}. 

Queried from a physical device /\+ surface pair to determine valid swapchain image counts, extents, and the formats and present modes the surface supports. Used by {\ttfamily \doxylink{class_lava_v_k_1_1_swap_chain}{Swap\+Chain}} when selecting swapchain creation parameters.
