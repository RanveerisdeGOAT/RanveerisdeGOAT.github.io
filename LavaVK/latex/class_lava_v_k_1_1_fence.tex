\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
CPU-\/\+GPU synchronization primitive. 

A \doxylink{class_lava_v_k_1_1_fence}{Fence} is used by the CPU to determine when GPU work has completed. Unlike semaphores, fences can be waited on and reset directly by the CPU.
