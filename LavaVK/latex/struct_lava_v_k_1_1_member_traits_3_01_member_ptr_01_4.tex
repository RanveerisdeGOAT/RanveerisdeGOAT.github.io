Compile-\/time helper that extracts the byte offset of a struct member from its pointer-\/to-\/member. 

Specializes \doxylink{struct_lava_v_k_1_1_member_traits}{Member\+Traits} for the case where {\ttfamily Member\+Ptr} is a pointer to a member {\ttfamily M} of struct {\ttfamily S}. Used by \+\#\+Vertex\+Layout::\+attribute() to compute a vertex attribute\textquotesingle{}s byte offset directly from a {\ttfamily \&Vertex::\+field} expression, without the caller needing {\ttfamily offsetof}.
