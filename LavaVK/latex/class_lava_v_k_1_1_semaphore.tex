\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
GPU-\/\+GPU synchronization primitive. 

A \doxylink{class_lava_v_k_1_1_semaphore}{Semaphore} is used to synchronize operations performed by GPU queues. Semaphores are commonly used when acquiring swapchain images, submitting command buffers, and presenting rendered images — e.\+g. an "{}image available"{} semaphore that a submit waits on before writing to a swapchain image, and a "{}render finished"{} semaphore that a submit signals and that the present call waits on.

Unlike \doxylink{class_lava_v_k_1_1_fence}{Fence}, semaphores cannot be waited on or reset directly by the CPU; they are only ever waited on /\+ signaled by GPU queue operations. {\ttfamily \doxylink{class_lava_v_k_1_1_semaphore}{Semaphore}} is move-\/only RAII:\+ the underlying {\ttfamily Vk\+Semaphore} is created in the constructor and destroyed in the destructor.
