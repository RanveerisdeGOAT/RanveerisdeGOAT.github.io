\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
GPU-\/\+GPU synchronization primitive. 

A \doxylink{class_lava_v_k_1_1_semaphore}{Semaphore} is used to synchronize operations performed by GPU queues. Semaphores are commonly used when acquiring swapchain images, submitting command buffers, and presenting rendered images.

Unlike \doxylink{class_lava_v_k_1_1_fence}{Fence}, semaphores cannot be waited on or reset directly by the CPU.
